Why this exists

The problem is not that the help is missing. It is that nobody can find it.

Ayushman Bharat and MJPJAY each cover ₹5 lakh per family per year. Between them they cover most families in Maharashtra. Yet people still sell land and take loans for treatment that was already paid for.

The reasons are ordinary. Nobody knows the scheme exists. Nobody knows which card colour qualifies. Nobody knows which document to carry, so they are turned away and do not come back.

This site tries to close that gap for one district at a time — plainly, in two languages, on a phone.
